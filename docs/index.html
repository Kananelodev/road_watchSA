RoadWatch SA

An AI agent built in Google AI Studio that turns vague pothole complaints into structured, prioritised repair reports for municipalities and road contractors.

Open the agent in AI Studio View the repo

The problem

Potholes burst tyres, damage cars and push taxis into oncoming lanes. Residents complain on WhatsApp ("massive hole near the Engen"), but those reports are vague and duplicated, and road crews can't tell which hole to fix first.

What the agent does

Resident mode

Asks up to 2 quick questions at a time (where, how big, how dangerous) in English, isiZulu, Sesotho and more, then writes a complete report with a danger score.

Responder mode

Takes a batch of messy complaints, merges duplicates, ranks them 0–100, groups nearby jobs into one crew trip, and flags missing details.

Structured output

Every reply can be one JSON object matching a strict schema, ready for a tracker, spreadsheet or map.

Example conversation

ResidentThere's a massive pothole near my place, nobody cares.
RoadWatch SAThat's frustrating. Where is it? A street name and a nearby landmark (shop, garage, taxi rank) is perfect.
ResidentVilakazi St, Orlando West, by the Engen near the taxi rank. Bigger than a tyre, full of water when it rains.
RoadWatch SAUrgent · 81 Major pothole on a busy taxi route, hidden by water. Here's your report; submit it to the JRA and your ward councillor.

Security & POPIA

✓ Guardrails

Top-priority rules in the prompt plus a code filter that blocks prompt injection and unsafe requests before they reach Gemini.

✓ No exposed keys

The key is read only from an environment variable. An automated scan runs on every push. No client-side code.

✓ POPIA

Anonymous by default, contact details only with consent, other people's details removed, nothing stored.
